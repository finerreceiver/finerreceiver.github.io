The warm optics of FINER, a coupling optical mechanism that picks up the radio waves collected by the LMT and leads them to the FINER receiver, is ready. Tatsuya Takekoshi at Kitami Institute of Technology is finalizing its integration.

FINER receiver used for a dark photon search (DOSUE-RR)

Before its shipment to the LMT, the FINER Band 6+7 receiver (210–350 GHz) is being used at NAOJ by DOSUE-RR, an experiment that searches for dark matter with radio waves. The team led by Okayama University, including Associate Professor Adachi, installed its own calibration and readout system on FINER and started the search for dark photons in the 0.9–1.4 meV mass range on 6 April 2026. See the announcement by Okayama University (in Japanese).

FINER project has started with ALMA2 receiver technology

The FINER project has entered its development phase. This week we signed a research agreement with the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ), under which FINER will use the receiver technologies developed for ALMA2, the next-generation upgrade of ALMA. Together with the Large Millimeter Telescope in Mexico, FINER aims at the highest spectral-scanning sensitivity in the northern hemisphere to identify distant galaxies that remain unidentified in the northern sky.
