FAQ

How can I use FINER?

FINER will be offered on the LMT through the regular LMT Call for Proposals after commissioning. Observing time on the LMT is allocated to the LMT partner communities, and eligibility to propose follows the rules of each Call; there is currently no formal route for proposers outside the LMT partnership.

When does FINER come online?

FINER is expected to come online in 2027, after installation and commissioning on the LMT.

Science

Galaxy formation in the first half of the epoch of reionization

Background

The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) has found more than a hundred candidate galaxies at redshifts z > 8, when the Universe was less than about 600 million years old, and has confirmed some of them spectroscopically out to z ≈ 14. The bright end of their ultraviolet luminosity function evolves surprisingly little with redshift (Harikane et al. 2022), which means that massive galaxies had to grow very efficiently within a short time. This places a stringent constraint on models of cosmic structure formation.
