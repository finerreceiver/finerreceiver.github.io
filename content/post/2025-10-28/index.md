We have released the LMT/B4R Commissioning and Science Observations Report in 2024.
B4R is a 2-mm dual-polarization 2SB heterodyne receiver system ([Kawabe et al. 2025, AJ, 170, 350](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ae12eb); [Yonetsu et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.21845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21845)) and the precursor of the FINER receiver system.
