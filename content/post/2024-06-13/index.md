A series of papers on FINER is now available on arXiv and was presented at SPIE Astronomical Telescopes + Instrumentation 2024 in Yokohama. Haoran Kang and Masato Hagimoto reported the development status of the receiver and the spectrometer.

- [Tamura et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07975): project overview
- [Kang et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07974): receivers
- [Hagimoto et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07978): digital spectrometer array
