### How can I use FINER?
FINER will be offered on the LMT through the regular [LMT Call for Proposals](http://lmtgtm.org/for-lmt-users/call-for-proposals/?lang=en) after commissioning. Observing time on the LMT is allocated to the LMT partner communities, and eligibility to propose follows the rules of each Call; there is currently no formal route for proposers outside the LMT partnership.

### When does FINER come online?
FINER is expected to come online in 2027, after installation and commissioning on the LMT.

### Can I join FINER development?
Yes. We welcome new collaborators in the development of FINER. Please contact the PI, Yoichi Tamura (Nagoya University; ytamura (at) nagoya-u.jp).
