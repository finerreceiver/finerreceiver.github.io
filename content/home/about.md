FINER (Far-Infrared Nebular Emission Receiver) is a dual-polarization sideband-separating superconductor-insulator-superconductor (SIS) mixer receiver for [the Large Millimeter Telescope](http://lmtgtm.org/) (LMT) in Mexico. By exploiting [ALMA](https://www.almaobservatory.org/)'s new wideband sensitivity upgrade (WSU) technology, FINER covers a wide range in radio frequencies from 120–350 GHz, with an instantaneous intermediate frequency (IF) of 4–20 GHz per sideband per polarization, providing 40% of ALMAʼs light-collecting area, a similar atmospheric transmittance to ALMA, and a 5 times wider bandwidth than the current ALMA, which offers the most sensitive spectral-scanning capability among millimeter and submillimeter telescopes in the northern hemisphere.

The laboratory integration and evaluation of the full FINER system were completed at the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ) in October 2026, and the system is being prepared for installation on the LMT. FINER is expected to come online in 2027.
